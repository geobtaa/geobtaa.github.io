    title: BTAA-GIN Work Featured at Fall Conferences
    intro: >-
      In fall of 2026, BTAA-GIN members presented the network's work at regional
      and national conferences serving data librarians, map librarians, and GIS
      professionals. Presenting at these events helps BTAA-GIN share its work,
      learn from peers, and strengthen connections across the geospatial and
      library communities.


      * Introducing BTAA-GIN: Jaime Martindale presented an overview of the consortium's collaborative programs, collections, and services at the *B1G Cartography Lab Workshop*.

      * BTAA-GIN Tools and Preservation Initiatives: Amanda Tickner (Michigan State University) and Nicole Scholtz (University of Michigan) presented the history and evolution of BTAA-GIN, including the Geoportal, the Data Citation Generator, and future work to preserve ephemeral geospatial datasets at the *CMPASS Educational Conference* in Kalamazoo.

      * Data Governance and Long-Term Geospatial Preservation: Jaime Martindale (University of Wisconsin-Madison) and Karen Majewicz (BTAA-GIN) participated in the panel discussion, "Do You Have a Roads Layer from 2000?", exploring approaches to data governance, preservation, and long-term access to geospatial data at *GIS-Pro* in Milwaukee.

      * Geospatial Data Archiving and Preservation: Karen Majewicz (BTAA-GIN, University of Minnesota) discussed community efforts, open-source tools, and long-term strategies for preserving public geospatial resources at *FOSS4G* in Sacramento.

      * BTAA Geospatial API: Eric Larson (BTAA-GIN) described the architecture and future applications of BTAA-GIN's next-generation geospatial platform at *FOSS4G*.

      * Data Citation Generator: Innocensia Achieng Owuor (Purdue University) demonstrated the BTAA-GIN Data Citation Generator and discussed how the tool helps address challenges in citing geospatial data accurately and consistently at the *Midwest Data Librarian Symposium* in West Lafayette, Indiana.

      * BTAA BIG Map Collection: Theresa Quill (Indiana University), Heather Ross (Penn State University), and Kathleen W. Weessies (Michigan State University) shared an update on the initiative and ongoing collaborative efforts to improve access to cartographic resources at the *Western Association of Map Libraries (WAML) Conference* in Denver.
