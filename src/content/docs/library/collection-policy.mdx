## Overview

The BTAA Geoportal is a platform that offers an enhanced single point of discovery and access to geographic and cartographic resources from:

* libraries that share geospatial data, scanned maps, and aerial imagery collections for broader discoverability and accessibility to a wide variety of users  
* the BTAA-GIN Geodata Collection Archive - a source of curated, digitally archived geospatial data focused on the Big Ten region   
* Open data portals and other public sources throughout the United States and internationally

## Collection Scope

BTAA-GIN aims to curate a collection of geospatial and cartographic content that is appropriate to the mission of the participating BTAA institutions. The collection includes resources and topics specifically relevant to faculty and student research interests.

### 1\. Resource Classes

The collection primarily consists of:

* raster and vector geospatial datasets
* scanned maps
* aerial imagery

The collection may also include:

* interactive applications (without downloadable data) such as StoryMaps or other web maps  
* websites that contain geospatial resources or maps not indexed at the item level in the Geoportal   
* collection-level records that aim to better organize information for users

### 2\. Geographic areas

The Geoportal emphasizes resources covering the Big Ten region while also including materials from elsewhere in the United States and internationally. Geographic coverage is informed by the collection strengths of participating libraries and the research and teaching interests of their communities.

### 3\. Access

The geoportal focuses primarily on discovery and access to open data. A select number of licensed resources are discoverable, but may be restricted to users affiliated with a participating institution.

## Collection Methods

### 1\. Metadata Indexing

Metadata records are harvested from a variety of sources, including libraries, open data portals and public websites.  Select metadata records from OpenGeoMetadata (a large online metadata collective primarily populated by academic institutions) are also indexed. These resources are limited in scope to those that offer file downloads, mapping applications, or web services immediately viewable or accessible to users. 

### 2\. Geodata Collection Archive

Select geospatial datasets from data providers within Big Ten states are collected and archived by BTAA-GIN at different time intervals and made available through the Geoportal. As archived resources offer more stable access over the long term, historical data relevant to research interests across the Big Ten will also be collected and archived. Priority is given to: (a) datasets that are especially relevant to research being contacted at Big Ten institutions, and (b) datasets that are at highest risk of disappearing and becoming inaccessible.

## Curation Principles and Management

### Audience

Resources are selected and maintained to support research, teaching, learning, and information needs of researchers at participating institutions, academia more broadly, and the public.

### Curation

BTAA-GIN may prioritize curated selections from larger collections or data portals rather than comprehensive indexing. Selection priorities may evolve in response to periodic collection assessments.

### Maintenance

BTAA-GIN periodically evaluates indexed and archived resources to improve discoverability, maintain access, and ensure continued relevance. Records may be updated, withdrawn, or reorganized when resources become unavailable, outdated, duplicative, or no longer align with collection priorities.

### Diversity

The Geoportal and its participating institutions aspire to collect and provide access to geospatial resources that represent diverse perspectives, abilities, and experience levels.
