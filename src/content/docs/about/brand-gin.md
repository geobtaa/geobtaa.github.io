## Logos

The primary logo combines the Big Ten Academic Alliance identity with the full Geospatial Information Network name. Use one of the supplied logo files rather than recreating the lockup in text.

### Standard logo

Use the standard black-and-blue logo on white and other light, uncluttered backgrounds.

<span class="gin-logo-preview gin-logo-preview--light">![Big Ten Academic Alliance Geospatial Information Network standard logo](@images/BTAA-Geospatial-logo.png)</span>

File: `BTAA-Geospatial-logo.png`

### White logo

Use the white logo on Dark Blue, Medium Blue, black, or another sufficiently dark and uncluttered background.

<span class="gin-logo-preview gin-logo-preview--dark">![Big Ten Academic Alliance Geospatial Information Network white logo](@images/BTAA-Geospatial-logo-white.png)</span>

File: `BTAA-Geospatial-logo-white.png`

### Logo use

- Preserve the logo's original 3:1 proportions.
- Leave the transparent space built into the supplied files intact whenever possible.
Do not recolor, crop, rotate, outline, rearrange, or add effects to the logo.

### Accessible color combinations

Preferred text combinations include:

| Text | Background | Contrast | Use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| White | Dark Blue | 11.68:1 | All text sizes |
| White | Medium Blue | 7.02:1 | All text sizes |
| Black | White | 21:1 | All text sizes |
| Black | Bright Blue | 5.41:1 | Normal and large text |
| Black | Green | 9.52:1 | Normal and large text |
| Black | Yellow | 16.26:1 | Normal and large text |

White text on Bright Blue does not provide enough contrast for ordinary body text. Use black text instead, or use Medium Blue or Dark Blue as the background.

## Typography

Use typography to establish a clear hierarchy without introducing additional decorative typefaces.

### Work Sans

Use **Work Sans** for:

- Document and presentation titles
- Section headings
- Slide headings
- Short labels, pull quotes, and calls to action
- Chart titles and important data labels

### Lora

Use **Lora** for:

- Body copy
- Introductions and explanatory text
- Reports and longer passages
- Quotations and captions when a more editorial tone is appropriate


### Font substitutions

When the preferred fonts are unavailable, use:

- **Arial**, **Helvetica**, or another clean sans serif in place of Work Sans
- **Georgia** or **Times New Roman** in place of Lora


## Naming

On first reference, use **Big Ten Academic Alliance Geospatial Information Network**. Use **GIN** on subsequent references. 

When describing the program, use **Big Ten Academic Alliance** rather than relying on the abbreviation BTAA when the audience may not recognize it.

When describing the **BTAA Geoportal**, the abbreviation BTAA is the most common form. It is also acceptable to describe it as the Geoportal, capitalizing the first letter.
